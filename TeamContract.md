## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will use Wechat for communication outside of class, when required for work on your course project. 

* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages in at most 1 day.

* A teammate notify if they think they won't be able to meet a deadline, and if they have to miss a TUT / Lec. At least 1 hr before an event.

* Respectful and inclusive behaviour are necessary for smooth and productive communication. Ex. Actively responding to teammates' ideas.
